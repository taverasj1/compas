Privacy Policy — Compás

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Compás is a choreography cue trainer for Latin dance. You place timed move cues on a track and hear them spoken aloud just before each move. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Compás has no accounts, no servers of our own, and no analytics or advertising. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. Everything you create stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Data stored on your device

Your cues, loop points, playback settings, saved choreographies, and — if you enter one — your Gemini API key are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. This data is never transmitted to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

YouTube

When you load a YouTube track, the app embeds Google's official YouTube IFrame player. Your use of that player is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Optional AI auto-cue (Gemini)

The AI auto-cue feature is optional and off until you provide a key. If you enter a Gemini API key, the app sends the loaded video's reference and your move vocabulary directly from your device to Google's Generative Language API to generate cue suggestions. We never receive that data. Alternatively, you may point the app at your own Cloud Function URL, in which case requests go to a server you control. Use of Google's API is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Local audio files

Audio files you open stay on your device and are played locally. They are never uploaded.

Children

Compás is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
